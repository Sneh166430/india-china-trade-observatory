Sustainability & Cost Analysis

Unsold stock is not necessarily textile waste. Fabric weight indicates material contained in projected unsold garments, not proven waste or environmental savings.

Research Evidence & Methodology

Research objective: Explore how simple demand estimates can help small apparel businesses assess production and inventory decisions.

Forecast method: Each future month's estimated demand equals previous-month demand multiplied by (1 + selected trend), compounded monthly.

Unsold estimate: Planned production minus first-month forecast demand, with a minimum of zero. The unsold share is calculated relative to planned production.

Limitations: This is a rule-based scenario calculator, not a trained machine-learning model. It does not yet account for seasonality, returns, existing inventory, promotions, lead times or uncertainty intervals. Its estimates have not been validated against real business data.

Next research phase: Test forecasts using anonymized historical sales data from consenting apparel SMEs, compare against baseline forecasting methods and report forecast error.
