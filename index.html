Research Questions

  1. How has India's merchandise trade balance with China evolved?
  2. Which product categories require closer supply-chain analysis?
  3. What challenges do Indian apparel SMEs face in cross-border sourcing?
  4. How can trade intelligence support SME business decisions?

Research Methodology

Research Design: Exploratory and descriptive secondary-data analysis.

Data Sources: Official merchandise trade statistics, including India's Export Import Data Bank and UN Comtrade.

Analysis: Import–export comparison, bilateral trade balance and qualitative assessment of SME implications.

Data Comparability: Calendar-year and fiscal-year figures must be analyzed separately. Source definitions, statistical revisions and valuation conventions may differ.

Limitations: Aggregate bilateral figures do not establish individual SME performance, product-level dependence or causal relationships.

Research Status: Interactive prototype. Official dataset compilation and validation pending.
